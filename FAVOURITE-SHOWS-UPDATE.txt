Umay TV Guide — Favourite Shows update

Added:
- Favourite Shows filter
- Add/remove a show as a favourite from the programme popup
- Favourites are saved on the phone
- Favourite programmes show a small heart in the guide
- Notification 10 minutes before each matching programme
- Notification when the programme starts
- Tapping the notification opens Umay TV Guide
- CBeebies fallback row retained
- Existing dark popup logo fix, English category labels and description fallback retained

Android 13+:
The app asks for notification permission the first time a show is added as a favourite.

Exact timing:
The app uses exact alarms when Android allows them and falls back to an idle-safe alarm otherwise.
