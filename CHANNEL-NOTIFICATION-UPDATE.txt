Umay TV Guide — channel-logo notification update

Added:
- Favourite-show notifications use the channel logo from guide.xml as the large notification icon.
- If the logo cannot be downloaded, the notification still appears normally.
- Android's required small/status-bar icon remains the app icon.
- Tapping a favourite-show notification opens Umay TV Guide directly to that programme's detail sheet.
- Works whether the app was closed or already open.
- Reminder metadata now includes channel ID, channel logo URL and exact programme start time.
- Existing 10-minutes-before and programme-start notifications remain.
- JVM Java/Kotlin target fixed to 17 in app/build.gradle.kts.
- Version bumped to 1.1 (versionCode 2).
